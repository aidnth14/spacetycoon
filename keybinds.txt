SPACE TYCOON — CONTROLS
=======================

MENU & NAVIGATION
-----------------
Left Click            Select buttons, text fields, or settings
Escape                Go back, open/close pause menu
Enter                 Confirm selections

IN-GAME (ONLINE / SOLO)
-----------------------
W A S D               Move (or use Arrow Keys)
L-Shift               Dash (Cooldown)
Space                 Jump
T or Enter            Open text chat (type message, press Enter to send, Esc to cancel)
V (Hold)              Push-to-Talk Voice Chat
Tab                   Cycle world rendering styles / palettes
R                     Regenerate world layout
- / =                 Zoom Out / Zoom In

IN-GAME (LOCAL CO-OP)
---------------------
Player 1: W A S D     (Move)   | Space  (Jump)
Player 2: Arrows      (Move)   | RShift (Jump)
Player 3: I J K L     (Move)   | O      (Jump)
Player 4: T F G H     (Move)   | Y      (Jump)

DEBUG & ENGINE
--------------
F1                    Toggle Debug Info (If implemented)
F2                    Force regenerate soundtrack (Assets)

GAMEPAD (Xbox / PlayStation style)
----------------------------------
D-Pad / L-Stick       Move menu focus
A Button              Confirm
B Button              Go back / close menus
